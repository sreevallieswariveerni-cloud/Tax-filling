Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains how Ledgerly may collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: October 2026

We aim to handle your personal and financial information responsibly and only use it for legitimate service-related purposes.

01

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Ledgerly, we may collect information such as:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Account details needed to provide access to our services.
  • Documents and financial information that you choose to submit for assistance.
  • Basic technical information relating to your use of our website.
02

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested services.
  • Manage accounts, documents and service requests.
  • Communicate updates and information relevant to your request.
  • Maintain website security and improve the user experience.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
03

When Information May Be Shared

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service providers or professional advisers when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, or where disclosure is required by law.

04

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No online system can guarantee complete security. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

05

Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and any records we are legally required to retain.

06

Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support its operation and remember settings. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain features may affect how the website works.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

08

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please contact the Ledgerly team.
