BEFORE YOU BEGIN

What might you need to prepare?

Requirements vary based on your income sources and individual circumstances. These are examples of information that may be relevant.

PAN and identity details
Salary or income statements
Bank and interest statements, where relevant
Details of eligible deductions, where applicable
Previous tax records, if available

Ask Our Team
What documents do I need for income tax filing?

It depends on your income sources and circumstances. Common examples include PAN details, income statements and relevant deduction records. The exact list should be confirmed for your case.

Can I ask for help before I start?

Yes. Contact Ledgerly to discuss your needs and understand the likely next steps.

Will every taxpayer need the same documents?

No. Requirements can differ according to income type, deductions and other individual circumstances.
